6 RECOMMENDATIONS & CONTRIBUTION
Finding Recommendation Next Action
Telemetry uses simulated data. Integrate physical temperature and GPS sensors. Connect IoT hardware through a secure telemetry gateway.
Validation was performed within the project environment. Conduct broader stakeholder validation. Pilot with rural healthcare clinics.
Further production hardening is required. Strengthen infrastructure and operational controls. Implement monitoring, backups, scalability and deployment controls.
Contribution

MedLine Rural contributes a workflow-oriented approach integrating medical inventory management, supply-request prioritisation, shipment coordination, driver operations, telemetry monitoring, cold-chain alerting and role-based security within one web-based system.
